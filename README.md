# Salaryman

You play as John Salaryman, who works at a large company. In order to climb through the corporate ladder and get promoted you must kill several bosses. Each promotion allows you to bring better and better gear with you to the office everyday using your increased salary. You can find more bosses by exploring more areas which you can get into using movement upgrades dropped by each boss upon their first death. The order in which you can fight most bosses is not set, with the difficulty of every area increasing every time a boss is killed.

### Proof Of Concept Test
We believe that we can get every area we conceptualized into full implementation, and if not, we can remove a area and spend that week cleaning all the other areas   
Our main risk is the scale, most of the core design of the game we already have down, the issue will be making different areas with different enemies that feel unique and fun to play through, and doing that with only one week per area  
We should be able to roughly design a area, with a boss, sound, some version of an enemy, varying rooms, and platforming sections within the week.   

### Proof Of Concept Results
Both of the members of the groups were both exceedingly busy this week and only had about 2 days total to create what we have added since the week before, and while we were not able to meet every criteria, notable missing out on a boss and room design being lackluster, the fact that this was done in 2 days of relatively calm work, implies that we should, in the event we are able to work throughout the week, design full in-depth areas which are unique through each week.    

#### Moving Forward
We believe that we can still implement every area we designed, however we plan for another check in around the halfway point to re visit the issue of potentially cutting out another area, however we both doubt this will be necessary, but we will be keeping the idea in mind
