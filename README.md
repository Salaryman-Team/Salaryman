# Salaryman

You play as John Salaryman, who works at a large company. In order to climb through the corporate ladder and get promoted you must kill several bosses. Each promotion allows you to bring better and better gear with you to the office everyday using your increased salary. You can find more bosses by exploring more areas which you can get into using movement upgrades dropped by each boss upon their first death. The order in which you can fight most bosses is not set, with the difficulty of every area increasing every time a boss is killed.
